SPECIAL ADVISORY

Stay hydrated, avoid prolonged exposure to peak heat, and follow official local advisories.

Recommended Precautions

  • Drink sufficient water.
  • Use shade and light clothing where appropriate.
  • Check heat alerts before outdoor activities.
  • Follow instructions from relevant authorities.
